About

Hi there! I'm Brittany, and I like building things. I'm a frontend engineer with expertise in crafting accessible, pixel-perfect user interfaces. I take pride in creating thoughtful, inclusive products and have a sharp eye for the little details that separate a good product from an exceptional one.

I enjoy working most at the intersection of design and engineering, where great user experience meets clean, scalable code.

Currently, I'm a Senior Frontend Engineer at Klaviyo , where I work on our design system and help build new products, like Composer . I lead engineering efforts across our component library and frontend platform, partnering closely with designers and engineers.

Previously, I’ve worked across a wide range of environments — from product studios to startups and large technology companies — including Apple, Starry Internet, and Upstatement.

Outside of my day-to-day work, I also created an online video course that walks through building a real-world, API-driven application from scratch.

In my spare time, you can usually find me climbing, playing tennis, spending time with my family, or exploring new places.
